02 / POLICY SWEEP

Randomness was not free

Kev greedy scored 43/56. With seeded sampling on the same model probabilities, temperatures 0.35, 0.70 and 1.00 scored 36, 26 and 29 out of 56 respectively. An abstention rule that required a 0.10 lead over the runner-up kept 33 decisions; 27 were correct. These were post-processing settings, not changes to weights or forward-pass speed. The 56 outputs are multiple views of 14 cases, not IID observations.

03 / NEXT CANDIDATES

What to test before racing the clock

Julia-1 and Laya are smaller constrained-choice candidates for the same harness. Kev 4B Q4 is a separately labelled quantized runtime, not an exact comparison with full-precision 4B. KaLM-Jev is another local choice scorer worth a separate check. None of these candidates is marked as measured here.

For a real match, keep game time moving, time-stamp every observation, and drop late model replies rather than applying an old decision to a changed board. No winner advances unless the game engine records an actual knockout.
