Rules and timing

Each of the five local checkpoints played with the same twenty controller configurations. Both sides received the same frame and legal option menu. The engine waited for both valid choices before advancing. There was no round timer: a verified knockout advanced the winner, a double knockout was replayed, and bracket byes were not wins.
