02 / METHOD

How the controllers work

Each model receives the current positions, distance, HP, energy, opponent action and readiness every six engine frames. At 60 frames per second that means a new choice every 100 ms. An input is only applied if the engine accepts it.

These are eight compact local decision trees fitted to different heuristic coaches, not GPT contestants or online learning. The models choose during play, rather than submitting a move list in advance. The native engine runs at a paced 60 fps; the replay samples positions every six frames.

Both seats start at 400 HP. The losing seat must reach zero or less. A 60-second technical watchdog can end a stalled bout as unresolved, never as a points win. The springboard benchmark below compares model choices with the same position and a changed guard state; it is not evidence that the models learned during a fight.
