02 / POLICY SWEEPHow sampling changed accuracy

Kev greedy scored 43/56. With seeded sampling on the same model probabilities, temperatures 0.35, 0.70 and 1.00 scored 36, 26 and 29 out of 56 respectively. An abstention rule that required a 0.10 lead over the runner-up kept 33 decisions; 27 were correct. These were post-processing settings, not changes to weights or forward-pass speed. The 56 outputs are multiple views of 14 cases, not IID observations.

04 / TWO-SEAT CANARYWhat happened with a reply deadline

Two controllers shared Julia-1, with a 60 Hz game clock and a 300 ms reply deadline. Both got fresh observations; expired replies did not replace active moves. These are tests only, not bracket results.

Open move menu. Unresolved: no engine-confirmed KO. Final HP 400 / 400. Accepted replies: 81 and 84. Native queued inputs: 324 and 332. Engine clock lateness p95: 0.17 ms. Chosen moves: DASH: 165.

Range-filtered move menu, 30 s limit. Unresolved: no engine-confirmed KO. Final HP 75 / 180. Accepted replies: 116 and 116. Native queued inputs: 104 and 146. Engine clock lateness p95: 0.15 ms. Chosen moves: CROUCH_B: 129, DASH: 46, FOR_JUMP: 18, STAND_FB: 18, STAND_B: 15, THROW_A: 2, FORWARD_WALK: 2, STAND_GUARD: 2.

Range-filtered move menu, extended. Engine-confirmed KO. Final HP 0 / 168. Accepted replies: 117 and 121. Native queued inputs: 122 and 163. Engine clock lateness p95: 0.46 ms. Chosen moves: CROUCH_B: 125, DASH: 55, STAND_FB: 20, STAND_B: 18, FOR_JUMP: 13, FORWARD_WALK: 4, STAND_GUARD: 2, THROW_A: 1.

What changed: with an open list both seats kept dashing without damaging the opponent. With range filtering, Kev-style model selection stayed in charge of choosing among viable actions. The extended trial ended in an actual KO. This is one canary, not a reliable win-rate estimate.

05 / CANDIDATE SOURCESSource projects and the match gate

Julia-1 and Laya are smaller constrained-choice candidates for the same harness. Kev 4B Q4 is a separately labelled quantized runtime, not an exact comparison with full-precision 4B. KaLM-Jev is another local choice scorer worth a separate check. The measured candidates appear in the comparison above. KaLM-Jev was not run in this pass.

For a real match, keep game time moving, time-stamp every observation, and drop late model replies rather than applying an old decision to a changed board. No winner advances unless the game engine records an actual knockout.
