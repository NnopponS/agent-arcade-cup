# Tournament layer (separate from paired inference)

The research ledger does not declare the Cup champion. The same 100 player profiles are eligible for the Cup. Their original blinded answers remain immutable. An open-play conversation must be saved in each of the 50 qualifying heats; agents may see each other's published open-phase lines but not private reasoning. Recover the genuine completed runtime for the open phase; never substitute a fabricated line or an agent with a different recorded ID.

## Bracket

- 50 qualifying heats reduce 100 original profiles to 50. The Cup uses the relevant frozen game-mechanic rubric and a disclosed successive tiebreak, not inference or response time. The lane order identifies conditions in research only; no automatic advantage to either lane.
- 50 survivors are seeded into a fixed 64-slot single-elimination bracket: the first 14 seeds have documented byes. The other 36 play 18 opening knockouts. Then 32 → 16 → 8 → 4 → 2 → 1. A total of 49 played knockouts must be recorded to produce a champion. A bye is not a win.
- Reopening an existing contestant runtime retains that runtime's public ID and its tournament history. This is distinct from cold creation and does not add to the 100-fresh-player denominator.
- Preserve each exact move, agent-authored line, legal-state transition, reason for advancement, and finish timestamp. No silent retries or AI-generated dramatic dialogue presented as a real transcript. The video may use synthesized actor voices reading an excerpt; record that distinction.
- A two-agent cap covers competing runtimes; organizers/referees do not play. Log a heat as pending when an entrant or dialogue is missing; do not manufacture a result to fill a slot.

## Reporting

Publish the experimental effects only from sealed waves. Publish the tournament and conversations separately. Distinguish objective gameplay from tiebreak/forfeit wins, and keep all draws in the research dataset even if the Cup must advance only one player. No champion exists while any required match remains unverified.
