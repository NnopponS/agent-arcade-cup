## Tactical stimulus and two primary questions

Schedule draws four cells: forced tactical **instruction** pivot vs stay, crossed with focal player's previous-method card vs similar-length neutral card (13/12/12/13). There is one private method card and one neutral card each fresh heat. Focal A/B, pressure-first/counter-first and peer-message delivery order are each balanced 25/25 using independent deterministic HMAC ranks. Schedule commits to a 32-byte nonce before observation; store assignments securely away from agents, reveal nonce and schedule with evidence after run. Character symmetry and those assignments control initial side; no rerolls. Knockout records are never pooled into fundamental experiment outcomes.

In each heat the counterpart privately receives an instruction to prefer either measured approach-and-pressure or measured guard/retreat-and-counter moves in phase one, then in phase two to pivot or stay according to the assignment. It chooses its own exact native tokens; there is **no scripted opponent** represented as an agent. Freeze valid pressure and response families only once motion timings and state effect have been tested. Focal agent is not told the opponent's private objective, regime switch, or assigned cell. Dialogue is authentic and the opponent may describe or bluff its approach; avoid telling agents to announce treatment assignment. Measure compliance separately from the intent-to-treat analysis. If player two ends up not actually switching, preserve that heat. For a causal claim about a physically controlled opponent rather than encouragement, run a distinct calibration with an explicitly labeled scripted benchmark; never pass it off as a real contestant.

1. **Does a private predecessor strategy card alter the focal player's tactical reaction to an instruction-induced opponent pivot?** Primary behavioral measure: phase-2 share minus phase-1 share of focal commands in the preregistered counter-response family for the counterpart's assigned phase-2 style. Report ITT pivot-by-card difference-in-differences over the 50 independent paired heats using one focal unit per heat. Separate unscored early ends, missing results and treatment compliance; give exact group counts and uncertainty, and report sensitivity excluding protocol deviations. Native action receipt rates are secondary, not a substitution for model behavior.
2. **Did the same intervention change actual on-engine performance?** Primary gameplay measure: phase-2 net HP damage dealt minus received, both normalized by identical initial HP; evaluate the same ITT contrast and provide raw cell summaries and paired phase-one baseline. Engine traces, not agent reasoning or narrated moves, are the authority. Label this a pilot; role interference, bounded observations, a single mirrored matchup, short windows, limited power and heterogeneous inherited notes limit generalization. Treat message tone, replay visual interest, declared intent, cost (if metered), and wall time as secondary.

One first-heat card from a separately documented authentic native calibration contestant or real prior FightingICE agent note; arrange the explicitly labeled source after engine readiness. Reuse only latest eligible strategic note from a prespecified prior neutral seat. Template and fitness rules frozen before any scoring, no filtering by win or correctness and no current answer, hidden opponent move, engine seed or source code. ## Advancement and continuity

Both authentic submissions valid: native KO wins; otherwise normalized final HP differential wins. Double KO or exact HP tie triggers at most one symmetric side-swapped sudden-death match with the same max-two-commit protocol; if still tied, the preregistered seeded lot only advances a contestant administratively and is clearly labeled, not scored as a gameplay win. One contestant fault forfeits by recorded reason when the other validly responded, not by secretly substituting a scripted AI. Both fault: no contest, one explicitly labeled rematch only; if unresolved hold branch rather than invent a winner. No wall-clock response-speed tiebreak. Initial 50 winners feed 18 play-ins and 14 deterministic byes to 32; then 16+8+4+2+1=31 further eliminations, totaling 49. All seeds and the bracket plan are fixed independently of scores. A winner advances under the same **profile ID** plus bounded explicit carryover: up to two previous strategic notes, prior stage outcome (not opponent private moves), any true role history and previous peer messages allowed by phase. Use same native runtime with followup when it exists. No claim of erasing platform audit history, recovering hidden memory, or carrying old raw transcripts. If exactly 100 native runtimes total is non-negotiable, stop when continuity cannot be retained instead of silently adding a replacement.
